# Position: ITC claimed on GSTR-2B basis (demo)

## The stand
Input tax credit is claimed in GSTR-3B only to the extent it appears in GSTR-2B for the period. Credit not in 2B is parked and claimed when it appears. [Source: partner decision, 2026-04-02]

## Scope
All monthly GST clients. Not applicable to RCM credit, which follows payment.
