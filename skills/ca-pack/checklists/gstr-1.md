# Checklist: GSTR-1 / IFF

Job opens 10 days before due.

1. [ ] Sales register for the period received (proof: file in `sources/`)
2. [ ] B2B invoices: GSTIN of every recipient validated against the client's master (proof: validation note, exceptions listed)
3. [ ] Credit and debit notes mapped to original invoices (proof: mapping table)
4. [ ] Exports, SEZ, deemed exports classified with shipping bill or LUT reference where applicable (proof: classification note)
5. [ ] HSN summary prepared at the digit level the client's turnover requires (proof: summary table)
6. [ ] Document series: numbering continuity checked, cancelled documents listed (proof: series table)
7. [ ] Amendments to earlier periods listed with reason (proof: amendment table or "none")
8. [ ] Totals agree to books and to the draft 3B outward figures (proof: tie-out line)
9. [ ] ◆ Reviewer says yes (proof: timeline)
10. [ ] ◆ Filed by a person · ARN recorded on client timeline (proof: ARN, date)
11. [ ] Lessons written if anything changed
