# Checklist: Tax audit (3CA/3CB and 3CD)

Job opens 60 days before due. Runs alongside `ca-audit-file` for the audit engagement.

1. [ ] Applicability confirmed with turnover and the relevant clause (proof: one line with law page link)
2. [ ] Signed financial statements or draft received (proof: `sources/`)
3. [ ] Every 3CD clause assigned an owner and a status on the job page (proof: clause table)
4. [ ] Clause workings: each clause either filled with a source or marked "not applicable, because" (proof: clause table complete)
5. [ ] Disallowances and reporting items cross-checked to the firm's positions where a stand is taken (proof: position links)
6. [ ] Related-party, loans, deposits and cash transactions schedules tied to books (proof: tie-out notes)
7. [ ] Management representations obtained via `templates/representation-letter` (proof: signed copy reference)
8. [ ] ◆ Reviewing partner says yes on the full 3CD (proof: timeline)
9. [ ] ◆ Uploaded and accepted by the client on the portal; UDIN generated by the signing CA and recorded (proof: client timeline)
10. [ ] ITR job linked and its due date confirmed (proof: link)
11. [ ] Lessons written if anything changed
